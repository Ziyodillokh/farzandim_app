### EN
```
Parvoz Growth is the CHILD-device part of the "Parvoz" family-safety system.
It is not a standalone app: a parent creates a child profile in the "Parvoz"
app and gets a 5-digit family code. When the child enters this code, the two
devices are linked.

WHAT THIS APP DOES (transparent monitoring):
After installation and parental consent, the app sends the following data to
the parent's "Parvoz" app:
• Current location and movement history (including in background) — for the map
• App usage time — for the screen-time report
• Daily step count (via Health Connect) — for activity statistics
• Device status (battery, model) and online/offline state
• Enforcement of app limits and schedules set by the parent
• Voice and video messages (recorded by the child) — for family messaging
• Optional, parent-enabled: "Uninstall protection" (off by default) — when on,
  the child cannot uninstall the app; the parent can turn it off at any time

OPEN AND HONEST:
• The app is NOT hidden: it is always visible via a persistent notification
  and its own icon. No spying or secret surveillance whatsoever.
• A clear consent screen is shown BEFORE any data is collected.
• Intended only to monitor your own child, with their awareness. It CANNOT be
  used to track any other person (e.g. a spouse).
• A child's data is never used for advertising or sold to third parties.
  All data is transmitted encrypted.

INSTALLATION:
The app is installed and configured by a parent/legal guardian on the child's
device. Setup requires a few system permissions (location, app usage,
notifications) — each is explained.
